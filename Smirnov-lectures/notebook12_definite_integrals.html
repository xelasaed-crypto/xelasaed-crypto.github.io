Riemann Sums
theory

Given f on [a, b], partition a = x₀ < x₁ < … < xₙ = b, choose sample points ξᵢ ∈ [xᵢ₋₁, xᵢ], define

Sₙ = ∑ f(ξᵢ) Δxᵢ,   Δxᵢ = xᵢ − xᵢ₋₁

If Sₙ tends to a limit as the mesh → 0, that limit is the definite integral.

Definite Integral
theory

The definite integral of f over [a, b] is

∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx = lim (mesh→0) ∑ f(ξᵢ) Δxᵢ

Geometric meaning: signed area between the graph of f and the x-axis on [a, b].

“Positive above the axis, negative below the axis.”
Connection with Antiderivatives
theory

If F'(x) = f(x), then

∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx = F(b) − F(a)

This is the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (to be developed in the next notebook).
