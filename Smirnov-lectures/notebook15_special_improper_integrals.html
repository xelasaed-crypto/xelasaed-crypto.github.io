Improper Integrals – Infinite Interval
theory

For ∫ₐ^∞ f(x) dx we define

∫ₐ^∞ f(x) dx = lim_{b→∞} ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx

If the limit exists (finite), the integral converges; otherwise it diverges.

Improper Integrals – Endpoint Singularity
theory

For ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx with a singularity at a, define

∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx = lim_{ε→0⁺} ∫_{a+ε}ᵇ f(x) dx

Similarly for singularities at b.

Typical Convergence Tests
theory
∫₁^∞ 1/x^p dx converges ⇔ p > 1
∫₀¹ 1/x^p dx converges ⇔ p < 1

These are the basic p-integrals used as comparison benchmarks.

Gaussian & Related
theory
∫_{−∞}^{∞} e^{−x²} dx = √π
∫₀^{∞} e^{−x} dx = 1
∫₀^{∞} x e^{−x} dx = 1!

These are prototypes for probability and gamma function.
